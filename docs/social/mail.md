# Mail

## How to play

**`/mail`** opens your mailbox. Mail is used to deliver items and messages
asynchronously — including auction-house payouts and undelivered trade goods.

{{ story "story/game-windows-mailboxoverlay--inbox-with-unread" caption="Mailbox — inbox with unread messages and attachments" }}

## Configuration

The mailbox has no dedicated YAML. Storage lives in the player file; delivery is
driven by other systems (e.g. the [auction house](auction-house.md)).
