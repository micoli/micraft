## 4. Playable classes

### Warrior

Specialist in melee combat and group protection. The only class able to equip heavy armor and shields.

**Class abilities:**
- `Second Wind` (passive, level 1): recovers 1d10 + level HP once per combat
- `Extra Attack` (active, level 5): attacks twice per round
- `Indomitable` (active, level 9): rerolls a failed saving throw, 1x/long rest

**HP progression**: d10 per level (best survivability)

### Mage

Massive ranged damage, area-control spells. Very fragile in melee.

**Class abilities:**
- `Magic Missile` (level 1): 1d4+1 force damage, automatic hit
- `Fireball` (level 5): 8d6 fire damage in a 6-block radius, DEX saving throw
- `Teleport` (level 9): instant teleportation up to 30 blocks, costs 30 mana

**HP progression**: d6 per level (lowest)

### Ranger

Combat/exploration hybrid. Monster-hunting specialist, bonus damage against creature types.

**Class abilities:**
- `Favored Enemy` (level 1): +2 damage against a chosen monster type
- `Arrow Rain` (level 5): attacks all enemies in a cone, costs 20 mana
- `Wild Senses` (passive, level 3): detects monsters within a 15-block radius

**HP progression**: d8 per level

### Rogue

Burst damage on a single target, stealth movement, and theft.

**Class abilities:**
- `Sneak Attack` (level 1): +1d6 damage if advantage or an ally is adjacent to the target
- `Shadow Step` (level 3): invisibility for 10 seconds, costs 15 mana
- `Evasion` (passive, level 7): no damage on a successful DEX saving throw (instead of half)

**HP progression**: d8 per level

### Cleric

Group support and healing, secondary combatant. The backbone of group play.

**Class abilities:**
- `Heal` (level 1): restores 1d8 + WIS HP to a target, costs 10 mana
- `Sanctify` (level 3): +1d4 radiant damage to all nearby allies for 30s
- `Resurrection` (level 9): revives a fallen ally with 1 HP, costs 50 mana, 1x/long rest

**HP progression**: d8 per level
