## 1. Vision & objectives

### Problem

The game is currently a multiplayer voxel sandbox with no game loop or progression. Players build freely in a persistent world but have no objectives, no reason to cooperate, and no structured content to explore.

### Vision

Transform the sandbox into a persistent multiplayer RPG of the dungeon & dragons type: exploration of procedural dungeons, class-based character progression, tactical combat, magical item crafting, and player-driven economy — while preserving the freedom of voxel construction.

### Measurable objectives

- A solo player can create a character, join a dungeon, and fight a boss in under 15 minutes
- A group of 4 players can complete a level-5 dungeon in 30 to 60 minutes
- 80% of active players have a level 5+ character after 3 play sessions
- The world remains persistent: dying in a dungeon does not erase out-of-dungeon progression

### Non-objectives (v1)

- Open-world PvP — too much balancing complexity for v1
- Marriage / advanced guild systems — to be addressed in v2
- Mounts and fast travel — traversing the voxel world is part of the gameplay
- Mobile touch interface — PC and browser first
