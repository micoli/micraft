## 12. Multiplayer & social

### Groups

- Maximum size: 6 players
- Creation: direct invitation by player name
- XP sharing: see section 5
- Loot: free-for-all by default, "round-robin" option activatable by the group leader
- Group resets if all members disconnect for more than 30 minutes

### Chat

- Global channel (whole world): limited to boss and PvP announcements (v2)
- Local channel (50-block radius): proximity communication
- Group channel: visible to group members only
- No voice chat in v1 (delegated to Discord/external tools)

### Economy

**Currency**: Gold (g). Obtained from quests, selling items to NPCs, farming.

**Auction house**: Asynchronous marketplace available in each major city.
- A player posts an item with a price
- Any other player can buy it
- 5% tax levied on each transaction
- Listings expire after 72h (item returned to seller)

**Direct trade**: Secure P2P trade interface between two nearby players (less than 5 blocks apart).
