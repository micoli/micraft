## 5. Progression & XP

### XP sources

| Action | XP gained |
|---|---|
| Kill a common monster | 50 × monster level |
| Kill an elite monster | 200 × monster level |
| Kill a dungeon boss | 1000 × dungeon level |
| Complete a side quest | 250 |
| Complete a main quest | 750 |
| Discover an unexplored location | 100 |
| Craft a rare item | 150 |

### Progression curve (total XP required per level)

```
Level 1 → 2  :    300 XP
Level 2 → 3  :    900 XP
Level 3 → 4  :  2,700 XP
Level 4 → 5  :  6,500 XP
Level 5 → 10 :  progressive escalation (×1.8 per level)
Level 10 → 20 : slow escalation (×1.4 per level)
```

Target pace: level 5 reachable in 6 to 8 hours of active play.

### Group XP sharing

When multiple players participate in combat:
- Total XP is divided by the number of participants
- A bonus of **+10% XP per additional group member** is applied (incentive for group play)
- Ex: 4 players kill a 1000 XP boss → 1000/4 = 250 + 30% bonus = **325 XP each**

### Rests

Rest system inspired by D&D 5e, adapted for persistent multiplayer:

**Short rest** (5 minutes idle, out of combat):
- Recovers 50% mana
- Recovers 25% HP
- Recharges abilities marked "short rest"

**Long rest** (sleeping in a bed, 8 in-game seconds = 1 night):
- Recovers 100% HP and mana
- Recharges all abilities
- Resets the daily dungeon counter
