  end of the run, after the game socket disconnected. Not reproduced in the rerun nor in two 4-traversal captures
  with console logging; no page error was recorded. Open: watch for it in the next runs.
- Idle took 4.3 min in the rerun instead of ~1.1 min (entry/settling, not the measured windows). Open.

- Traverse: p50 16.7 ms (60 FPS), p95 19.7 ms (51 FPS), p99 22.8 ms (44 FPS), max 31.5 ms (32 FPS), long frames
  0.27 % (0.48 % before).
- All deltas against `8ca3746c` within noise: no frame-time regression from sectioned chunks (the mesher reads
  through `SectionedBytes.get`).

  initial load.

### Server tick
- p50 ~1.0 ms idle and traverse, p99 4.0 / 5.1 ms — back to the level of the `33659457` session (0.88 ms) from the
  2.6 ms of the `8ca3746c` session. Part may come from cheaper chunk work (`topY` no longer scans 262 k cells,

  reliable (see the skill); a back-to-back run would settle it.

2. **No frame-time cost**: CPU remains dominated by per-mesh Babylon work (see `cpu/`), not by chunk storage.
3. **An intermittent page reset during traverse** (once in four runs) needs watching.
