## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make Faction alliance a constructor dependency of `ClaimRegistry` and delete the post-construction
`bindFactions`.

**Current behavior:**
`ClaimRegistry.factionAlly` is a `var` that defaults to "never an ally" and is set later by `bindFactions(factions)`.
A World built without that call silently denies Claim access to Faction allies, and the `Authorizer` has no way
to tell.

**Desired behavior:**
`ClaimRegistry` gets its Faction-alliance check (a `FactionManager` or an `(actorId, ownerId) -> Boolean`) at
construction. It is never mutable and there is no "unbound" state. Construction order in `buildGameWorld` creates
the `FactionManager` first.

**Key interfaces:**
- `ClaimRegistry` constructor: takes the alliance check
- `bindFactions`: deleted
- `Authorizer`: unchanged behaviour

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `ClaimRegistry` has no mutable alliance field and no `bindFactions`
- [ ] A test on a `buildGameWorld` World: a Faction ally of the Claim owner is granted access through the Faction
      cascade
- [ ] `make dc CMD="./gradlew :server:test"` green

**Out of scope:**
- Other rules in the `Authorizer`
