## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make `buildGameWorld` wire every subsystem exactly as the production World does, with persistence as
the only switch between a persistent and a memory-only World, and pin each hook with a behavioural test.

**Current behavior:**
`buildGameWorld(id, generator, shared, opts)` builds Test worlds and the simulator's arena, and describes itself as
"memory-only, additive, duplicates the Koin wiring". It diverges from production:
- `opts.persistence` only reaches `WorldState`: `PlayerPersister`, the Instance/Claim/ActionBlock/Scene/Guild
  registries, `ClaimManager` and `FactionManager` get `null`, and vegetation saves under `_e2e_discard`.
- No `AuctionManager`. `MailManager` exists only when `opts.persistence` is set.
- `IntentCollector` has no `onConsumeItem`, so consuming an item does nothing.
- `GuildManager` has no `returnBankItems`, so a dissolved guild's bank is lost.
- `FactionManager` applies default `FactionsSection()` instead of the server's factions config.
- NPC definitions load without the loot validator, and `NpcTickContext` ignores `MICRAFT_NPC_SEED`.
- `CombatProcessor` has neither `factionManager` nor `onPlayerDownedByNpc` (the NPC gains no XP).
- `BlockBreaker` weapon/tool registries are snapshots.

**Desired behavior:**
Rule: **when production and `buildGameWorld` differ, production wins.** Only persistence and the explicit options
(`tickSections`, `spawnEditMode`, `npcAutoSpawn`, clock, NPC hook decorator, `onCommand`) may vary.
- **Persistence is the one switch.** A non-null `WorldPersistence` gives a persistent World: real `PlayerPersister`,
  persisted registries, ClaimManager/FactionManager/GuildManager with persistence, vegetation saved in the World's
  directory, and an `AuctionManager`. Null gives a memory-only World with none of that persisted and no auction house.
- **`MailManager` always exists** (in memory when persistence is null) and has the `QuestManager`. Guild bank return
  goes through it.
- **The Faction config is process-level**: it lives in `SharedGameServices`, every World applies it, and a factions
  reload reaches every World.
- Consuming an item, NPC loot validation, `MICRAFT_NPC_SEED`, `onPlayerDownedByNpc` and the Faction friendly-fire
  rule behave as in production.

**Key interfaces:**
- `GameWorldOptions.persistence`: no longer a "test-only escape hatch" but the persistent/memory-only switch
- `SharedGameServices`: gains the factions section (and its reload)
- `MailManager`: must accept a memory-only store
- `GameWorld` exposes the subsystems it owns (mail, auction, quest…) for the later steps

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] One behavioural test per aligned hook, on a World from `buildGameWorld`:
  - NPC kill gives player XP, quest credit and loot
  - An NPC that downs a player gains XP
  - A mail attachment claim gives quest credit
  - Consuming an item applies its effect
  - A dissolved guild's bank is mailed to its owner
  - `/rest` clears regen accumulators
  - Same-Faction friendly fire follows the config
  - A tool reload is seen by block breaking
- [ ] A test builds a World with persistence, saves and rebuilds it, and finds its player, claim and registry state
- [ ] Existing Test world, simulator and `NpcTickParityTest` tests stay green unchanged
- [ ] `make dc CMD="./gradlew :server:test"` green; `make e2e` green

**Out of scope:**
- Building the production World with `buildGameWorld` ([14](14-default-world-from-builder.md))
- Changing the `CommandContext` shape ([10](10-command-context-deps.md))
